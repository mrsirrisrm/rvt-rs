- **Duct and pipe sizes on Revit 2024.** The curve-field layout of RE-134
  (a duct's or pipe's width, height and type, and an axis-aligned duct's
  length and ends) is read on Revit 2024 as on 2025. On the BIM4LCA
  housing's HVAC model (2024) 515 of 701 ducts are sized and the 488 with a
  length match MagiCAD's exported `Pituus` to 1 mm; on Snowdon Towers HVAC,
  1,037 of 1,053 are sized. Before, no 2024 duct had a size.
